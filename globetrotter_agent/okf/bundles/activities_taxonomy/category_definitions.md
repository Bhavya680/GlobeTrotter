# Category Definitions

1. **Sightseeing**: landmarks, monuments, museums, viewpoints, architecture.
   - Examples: Eiffel Tower, Colosseum, Shibuya Crossing.
   - Typical cost: $0-50 | Typical duration: 1-3 hours.
2. **Food**: restaurants, street food, food tours, cooking classes, markets.
   - Examples: Ramen tour, Pasta making, Night market.
   - Typical cost: $5-80 | Typical duration: 1-4 hours.
3. **Adventure**: hiking, water sports, extreme activities, outdoor experiences.
   - Examples: Surfing, Hiking, Desert safari.
   - Typical cost: $30-200 | Typical duration: 2-8 hours.
4. **Culture**: theatre, concerts, festivals, local ceremonies, art galleries.
   - Examples: Kabuki theatre, Opera, Flamenco show.
   - Typical cost: $20-150 | Typical duration: 2-4 hours.
5. **Shopping**: markets, malls, boutiques, artisan shops.
   - Examples: Grand Bazaar, Night Bazaar, SoHo.
   - Typical cost: varies | Typical duration: 1-3 hours.
6. **Wellness**: spa, yoga, meditation, thermal baths, retreat.
   - Examples: Balinese spa, Hammam, Onsen.
   - Typical cost: $20-200 | Typical duration: 1-3 hours.
