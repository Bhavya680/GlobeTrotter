# Duration and Pacing

- **Volume**: Recommend 2-3 major activities max per day.
- **Rhythm**: Structure as Morning / Afternoon / Evening activity rhythm.
- **Over-scheduling**: Advise users who over-schedule to leave buffer time for travel and spontaneity.
- **Recovery**: Allow recovery time between intense activities, especially for families or older travelers.
- **Day 1 arrival tip**: Don't over-schedule, buffer for jet lag and hotel check-in.
- **Last day departure tip**: Keep it light, account for airport transfer time.
