# Frequently Asked Questions

1. **How do I delete a trip?** Go to your Trip List, click the Delete button next to the trip, and confirm in the modal.
2. **Can I have more than one trip at the same time?** Yes, you can plan overlapping trips.
3. **What is a trip stop?** A distinct city or location within your broader trip.
4. **How are trip statuses calculated?** Automatically based on the start and end dates relative to today.
5. **Can I edit an itinerary after creating it?** Yes, via the Itinerary Builder.
6. **What is the Community tab for?** Sharing tips, itineraries, and experiences with other travelers.
7. **How do I make a trip public?** Toggle the visibility setting in your trip details to Public.
8. **What currencies does GlobeTrotter support?** Currently USD as the primary reference, though others can be noted.
9. **Can I copy someone else's itinerary?** Yes, if they have made it public.
10. **How do I add more stops to an existing trip?** Use the "Add Stop" button in the Itinerary Builder.
11. **What is the cost index?** A 1.0 to 10.0 scale indicating the relative expense of a destination.
12. **How is the budget calculated?** By summing the estimates across Transport, Stay, Activities, Meals, and Misc.
13. **Can I download my itinerary?** Currently available as a web view, print-to-PDF is supported by browsers.
14. **What is the calendar view for?** Visualizing your trips and activities over a month or week layout.
15. **Can I filter community posts?** Yes, by tags and popularity.
16. **How do I remove an activity from a stop?** Click the remove/trash icon next to the activity in the builder.
17. **What happens if my actual cost exceeds my budget?** The budget charts will highlight the overage, prompting cost-saving adjustments.
18. **Can I set a trip as private after making it public?** Yes, simply toggle the visibility back to Private.
19. **How do I change my profile photo?** Navigate to Profile -> Settings and upload a new photo.
20. **What does 'Copy Trip' do?** It duplicates a public itinerary into your own account for editing.
21. **How do I reset my password?** Use the 'Forgot Password' link on the login screen.
22. **Are my private trips visible to admins?** Admins can only see metadata for moderation, not the detailed content.
23. **Can I collaborate on a trip with a friend?** Collaboration is currently achieved by sharing a public copy, though real-time editing is not yet supported.
24. **How do I contact support?** Through the help section or by emailing support@globetrotter.com.
25. **What happens when I delete my account?** All your trips, posts, and data are permanently removed.
26. **Can I change the date of a stop?** Yes, as long as it remains within the overall trip's date range.
27. **Do you book flights?** No, GlobeTrotter is a planning tool, not a booking engine.
28. **How accurate are the activity suggestions?** They are based on general taxonomy and seed data.
29. **Can I add custom activities?** Yes, you can manually enter activity details.
30. **Is there a mobile app?** The web app is fully mobile responsive.
