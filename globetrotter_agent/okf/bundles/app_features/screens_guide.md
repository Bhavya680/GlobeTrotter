# Screens Guide

This is the agent's map of the application.

1. **Login (Screen 1)**: Allows users to log in with their email and password. Leads to Dashboard.
2. **Registration (Screen 2)**: For new users to create an account. Leads to Login or Dashboard.
3. **Dashboard (Screen 3)**: The main landing page showing active trips and quick actions. Leads to Create Trip, Trip List, etc.
4. **Create Trip (Screen 4)**: Form to start a new trip with dates and basic info. Leads to Itinerary Builder.
5. **Itinerary Builder (Screen 5)**: The core tool to add stops, activities, and budget sections. Leads to Itinerary View.
6. **Trip List (Screen 6)**: Displays all of the user's trips. Leads to Itinerary View or Edit.
7. **Profile (Screen 7)**: User settings, saved destinations, and history. Leads from Dashboard/Nav.
8. **Search (Screen 8)**: Global search for cities, activities, or community posts.
9. **Itinerary View (Screen 9)**: Read-only or detailed view of a planned trip with budget tracking. Leads to Share or Edit.
10. **Community (Screen 10)**: Space for users to share and view posts, reviews, and linked trips.
11. **Calendar (Screen 11)**: Visual month/week view of trips and activities.
12. **Admin (Screen 12)**: Admin panel for managing users and viewing analytics.
