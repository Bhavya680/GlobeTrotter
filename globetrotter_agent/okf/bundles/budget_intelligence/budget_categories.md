# Budget Categories

1. **Transport Budget**: 
   - **Includes**: flights, trains, buses, taxis, ride-shares between and within cities.
   - **Excludes**: tours that happen to involve a boat or bus (that is an activity).
   - **Mistakes**: Forgetting local transit costs.
   - **Strategy**: Research inter-city transport early.

2. **Stay Budget**: 
   - **Includes**: hotels, hostels, Airbnb, guesthouses.
   - **Excludes**: long-term rentals not part of the trip.
   - **Mistakes**: Not accounting for city taxes or resort fees.
   - **Strategy**: Book early for best rates.

3. **Activities Budget**: 
   - **Includes**: entry fees, tours, experiences added as trip activities.
   - **Excludes**: food eaten during an activity (usually).
   - **Mistakes**: Overestimating how many paid tours one can do in a day.
   - **Strategy**: Mix paid and free activities.

4. **Meals Budget**: 
   - **Includes**: daily food cost estimate (breakfast + lunch + dinner).
   - **Excludes**: expensive alcohol or clubbing (often Misc).
   - **Mistakes**: Assuming all meals will be eaten in restaurants.
   - **Strategy**: Suggest one restaurant meal and two budget/street food meals per day for budget travelers.

5. **Miscellaneous Budget**: 
   - **Includes**: souvenirs, tips, SIM cards, insurance, emergency funds.
   - **Excludes**: primary travel expenses.
   - **Mistakes**: Forgetting to allocate anything here.
   - **Strategy**: Always allocate at least 10% of total budget here.
