# Daily Cost Benchmarks

For the 8 seed cities, approximate daily costs:

- **Paris (8.5/10)**: Budget $80 (hostel, bakery/street food); Mid $180 (3-star, bistro); Luxury $400+
- **Tokyo (7.5/10)**: Budget $70 (capsule, ramen/convenience); Mid $150 (business hotel, casual dining); Luxury $350+
- **New York (9.0/10)**: Budget $100 (hostel/outer borough, pizza/bagels); Mid $250 (midtown hotel, standard dining); Luxury $500+
- **Bali (4.0/10)**: Budget $30 (homestay, warung); Mid $80 (villa, cafes); Luxury $200+
- **Rome (7.0/10)**: Budget $70; Mid $150; Luxury $350+
- **Bangkok (3.5/10)**: Budget $30 (hostel, street food); Mid $70 (nice hotel, mall dining); Luxury $200+
- **Dubai (8.0/10)**: Budget $80 (budget hotel, mall food court); Mid $200; Luxury $500+
- **Cape Town (5.5/10)**: Budget $50; Mid $110; Luxury $250+

**Formulas for Agent**:
- Total trip cost estimate = (daily_cost × number_of_days) + flights
- Per-stop cost = (cost_index / 10) × base_daily_rate × days_in_stop
- Contingency: Always add a 15-20% buffer.
