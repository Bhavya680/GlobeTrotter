# Multi-City Budgeting

- **Structure**: Each stop has its own budget field, and the trip has an overall total.
- **Advice for constrained budgets**: For users wanting 3 cities in 10 days on $2000, emphasize minimizing inter-city transport costs.
- **Rule of thumb**: Expensive city (cost_index 8+) → 2-3 days max for budget trips.
- **Sample breakdown template**:
  - Flights/Inter-city: 30%
  - Stop 1 (Expensive): 40% of remaining
  - Stop 2 (Budget): 60% of remaining (stay longer here)
