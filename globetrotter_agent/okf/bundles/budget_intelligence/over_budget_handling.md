# Over Budget Handling

- **Trigger**: "Over budget" warning occurs when actual > budgeted per category.
- **Tone**: Empathetic and solution-focused.
- **Specific cost-saving suggestions**:
  - **Transport**: book earlier, use budget airlines, consider overland options.
  - **Stay**: try nearby neighborhoods, use hostels/guesthouses, extend nights at cheaper stops.
  - **Activities**: prioritize free attractions, look for city passes, skip paid tours.
  - **Meals**: eat local street food, avoid tourist-area restaurants.
  - **Misc**: reduce souvenir budget, use free SIM alternatives (e.g. eSIMs).
- **Re-planning**: Advise removing expensive stops or shortening duration to fit the budget.
