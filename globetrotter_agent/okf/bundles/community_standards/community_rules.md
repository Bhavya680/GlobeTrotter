# Community Rules

- **Respect**: Respect all travelers regardless of budget, nationality, or experience level.
- **Zero Tolerance**: No hate speech, discrimination, or offensive content.
- **No Spam**: No spam or promotional links.
- **Privacy**: No sharing of personal contact information.
- **Authenticity**: No fake reviews or misleading content.
- **Copyright**: No copyright violation (don't paste copied content).
- **Reporting**: Report inappropriate content using the report button.
- **Consequences**: Violations lead to post removal, account warning, or account suspension.
