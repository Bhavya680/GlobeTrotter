# Engagement Tips

- **Search/Filter**: Search and filter to find relevant posts (by city, tag, popularity).
- **Likes**: Like posts you found helpful to boost their visibility.
- **Comments**: Comment with additional tips or questions.
- **Sharing**: Share posts on social media using the share button.
- **Copying**: Copy itineraries from public posts to start your own planning.
- **Follow Up**: Follow up on popular posts with your own experience.
