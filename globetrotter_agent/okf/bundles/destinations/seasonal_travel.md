# Seasonal Travel

- **Best and worst times**: Vary by region. E.g., Europe is best in shoulder seasons (Spring/Fall); Southeast Asia is best Nov-Feb.
- **Peak vs Off-season**: Peak season can inflate prices by 50-100% and guarantee crowds.
- **Weather Impacts**: Monsoons in Asia, hurricane season in the Caribbean, and extreme summer heat in the Middle East severely impact travel.
- **Golden window**: The 3-4 weeks just before or after peak season when weather is still good but crowds and prices drop.
- **Advising Users**: If dates fall in unfavorable seasons, advise on indoor activities, packing correctly, and highlight the cost savings to soften the blow.
