# Seed Cities Knowledge

## Paris
- **Description**: The romantic capital of France, known for its art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture. Its 19th-century cityscape is crisscrossed by wide boulevards and the River Seine.
- **Cost Index**: 8.5/10 = expect $150-250/day for mid-range travel.
- **Best time to visit**: April to June, or October to early November.
- **Top 3 activities**: Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum, Seine River Cruise.
- **Getting around**: Extensive Metro system; very walkable in the center.
- **Typical trip length**: 3 to 5 days.
- **Visa notes**: Schengen visa applies.
- **Hidden gem tip**: Explore the covered passages (Passages Couverts) for unique shopping.

## Tokyo
- **Description**: Japan's busy capital mixes the ultramodern and the traditional, from neon-lit skyscrapers to historic temples.
- **Cost Index**: 7.5/10 = expect $100-200/day for mid-range.
- **Best time to visit**: March to April (cherry blossoms) and September to November.
- **Top 3 activities**: Shibuya Crossing, Senso-ji Temple, Tsukiji Outer Market.
- **Getting around**: Highly efficient but complex subway and JR train networks.
- **Typical trip length**: 5 to 7 days.
- **Visa notes**: Many nationalities get a 90-day visa-free entry.
- **Hidden gem tip**: Visit Yanaka for a glimpse of old Tokyo.

## New York
- **Description**: The most populous city in the US, known for its skyline, Wall Street, Broadway, and diverse culture.
- **Cost Index**: 9.0/10 = expect $200-300/day for mid-range.
- **Best time to visit**: April to June and September to early November.
- **Top 3 activities**: Statue of Liberty, Central Park, Empire State Building.
- **Getting around**: 24/7 Subway system and walking.
- **Typical trip length**: 5 days.
- **Visa notes**: ESTA or US tourist visa required for most.
- **Hidden gem tip**: Walk the High Line early in the morning before crowds arrive.

## Bali
- **Description**: An Indonesian island known for its forested volcanic mountains, iconic rice paddies, beaches, and coral reefs.
- **Cost Index**: 4.0/10 = expect $40-80/day for mid-range.
- **Best time to visit**: April to October (dry season).
- **Top 3 activities**: Uluwatu Temple, Sacred Monkey Forest, Tegallalang Rice Terrace.
- **Getting around**: Scooters, taxis, or hired drivers.
- **Typical trip length**: 1 to 2 weeks.
- **Visa notes**: Visa on Arrival available for many countries.
- **Hidden gem tip**: Visit Sidemen Valley for quiet rice terraces instead of Ubud.

## Rome
- **Description**: Italy's capital, a sprawling, cosmopolitan city with nearly 3,000 years of globally influential art, architecture, and culture.
- **Cost Index**: 7.0/10 = expect $120-180/day for mid-range.
- **Best time to visit**: Mid-March to May, and September to November.
- **Top 3 activities**: Colosseum, Vatican Museums, Trevi Fountain.
- **Getting around**: Buses, limited metro, and extensive walking.
- **Typical trip length**: 3 to 4 days.
- **Visa notes**: Schengen visa applies.
- **Hidden gem tip**: Explore the neighborhood of Testaccio for authentic Roman food.

## Bangkok
- **Description**: Thailand's capital, known for ornate shrines and vibrant street life. The boat-filled Chao Phraya River feeds its network of canals.
- **Cost Index**: 3.5/10 = expect $30-70/day for mid-range.
- **Best time to visit**: November to February (cool and dry).
- **Top 3 activities**: Grand Palace, Wat Arun, Chatuchak Weekend Market.
- **Getting around**: BTS Skytrain, MRT subway, and river boats.
- **Typical trip length**: 3 to 5 days.
- **Visa notes**: Visa exemption for many countries for 30-45 days.
- **Hidden gem tip**: Take a longtail boat through the Thonburi canals.

## Dubai
- **Description**: A city and emirate in the UAE known for luxury shopping, ultramodern architecture, and a lively nightlife scene.
- **Cost Index**: 8.0/10 = expect $150-250/day for mid-range.
- **Best time to visit**: November to March.
- **Top 3 activities**: Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall, Desert Safari.
- **Getting around**: Metro, taxis, and ride-hailing apps.
- **Typical trip length**: 3 to 4 days.
- **Visa notes**: Visa on arrival for many western nationalities.
- **Hidden gem tip**: Explore the Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood.

## Cape Town
- **Description**: A port city on South Africa's southwest coast, on a peninsula beneath the imposing Table Mountain.
- **Cost Index**: 5.5/10 = expect $80-130/day for mid-range.
- **Best time to visit**: Late January to late April.
- **Top 3 activities**: Table Mountain Cableway, Robben Island, Cape of Good Hope.
- **Getting around**: Uber is the safest and most reliable option.
- **Typical trip length**: 5 to 7 days.
- **Visa notes**: 90-day visa-free entry for many countries.
- **Hidden gem tip**: Visit the Kirstenbosch Botanical Gardens on a Sunday for summer concerts.
