# Visa and Entry Basics

- **Disclaimer**: This is general info, not legal/official advice.
- **Common visa-free agreements**: EU Schengen area, ASEAN agreements.
- **Key things to know before booking**: Processing times can take weeks; some countries require proof of onward travel.
- **Passport validity rules**: Most countries require at least 6 months of passport validity beyond the date of entry.
- **Agent Policy**: Always answer visa questions by strongly recommending the user check the official embassy website or use an official portal.
