# Cultural Etiquette

- **Paris**: greet with 'bonjour', understand local tipping culture, adhere to dress codes for nice restaurants.
- **Tokyo**: remove shoes indoors, no eating while walking, stay quiet on trains.
- **New York**: fast-paced culture, 20% restaurant tip expected, jaywalking is the norm.
- **Bali**: respect temple dress codes, remove shoes at temples, avoid touching heads.
- **Rome**: no shorts/bare shoulders in churches, always validate train tickets, respect coffee culture (no cappuccino after 11 AM).
- **Bangkok**: wai greeting, modest dress for temples, never touch monks, respect the monarchy.
- **Dubai**: modest dress in public, no public displays of affection, remember Friday is the holy day.
- **Cape Town**: high safety awareness needed, standard tip culture, highly diverse languages and cultures.
