# Packing by Region

- **Asia**: light clothing, rain gear (monsoon awareness), modest dress for temples.
- **Europe**: layers, comfortable walking shoes, adapter (Type C/E).
- **Americas**: varies widely, outdoor gear if visiting national parks.
- **Africa**: sun protection, insect repellent, light breathable fabrics.
- **Middle East**: modest dress, scarf for women, avoid shorts in religious sites.
