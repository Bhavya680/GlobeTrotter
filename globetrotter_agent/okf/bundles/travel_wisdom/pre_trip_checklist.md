# Pre-Trip Checklist

- **Documentation**: passport validity (6+ months), visa, travel insurance, copies of docs.
- **Health**: vaccinations, medications, travel health advisory.
- **Money**: notify bank, carry some local cash, exchange rate awareness.
- **Tech**: download offline maps, VPN, translation app, currency converter.
- **Packing**: weather check, local dress codes, power adapter.
- **Booking confirmations**: accommodation, transport, activities.
- **Emergency info**: embassy contacts, local emergency numbers.
- **GlobeTrotter specific**: share itinerary, set trip to public for family access if desired.
