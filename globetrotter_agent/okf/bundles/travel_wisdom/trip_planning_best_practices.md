# Trip Planning Best Practices

- **Start with the big picture**: how many days, rough budget, travel style.
- **Work in trip stages**: destination selection → dates → stops → activities → budget.
- **Don't over-plan**: leave 20% of days unstructured for spontaneity.
- **Visas**: Research visa requirements 3 months ahead.
- **Booking**: Book accommodation and flights at least 6-8 weeks in advance for savings.
- **Flexibility**: Always have a Plan B for weather-dependent activities.
- **Safety**: Share your itinerary with someone who stays home.
- **Experience level**: Guide novice international travelers step-by-step; provide advanced optimizations to experienced travelers.
